## Scope and method

- FAQs were collected from each insurer's public website using HTTP fetches and, where JavaScript, 403 responses or TLS issues blocked plain requests, a real browser.
- Server-rendered FAQ pages were crawled individually; accordions, `<details>` elements, question headings, JSON-LD `FAQPage` blocks and bold-question paragraphs were parsed.
- Where an insurer publishes no dedicated FAQ, the closest public support Q&As are included and the limitation is noted.
- Answers are reproduced as published. They are not legal or financial advice; cover is governed by the current PDS/SPDS.

## Insurers with limited or no public FAQs

- **Vero** (0 Q&As): No public consumer FAQ page was found. Vero distributes through brokers; its public help/support pages cover domestic and family violence, financial hardship and additional resources, but do not publish a Q&A FAQ.
- **Youi** (0 Q&As): No dedicated public FAQ page was found. Youi publishes car, home and motorcycle articles and guides, including a 'Top 5 questions about motorcycle insurance' article, but no general FAQ Q&A page.
- **TIO** (0 Q&As): No public FAQ page was found on tiofi.com.au. TIO publishes customer support and contact pages, but no Q&A FAQ.
- **Acenda** (6 Q&As): No dedicated FAQ page was found. Six Q&As were extracted from Acenda's customer support pages (top support requests and identity-theft guidance).
- **Southern Cross Travel Insurance** (10 Q&As): The main /faqs page provides 10 Q&As. Individual FAQ URLs listed in the sitemap return blank/404 pages, so only the main page is included.
- **Travel Insurance Direct** (7 Q&As): Only eight Q&As were available on the current homepage. Legacy /customer-service FAQ pages redirect to nib.travelinsurancedirect.com.au or return 404.
- **Asteron Life** (16 Q&As): Sixteen claims Q&As were extracted from the public claims FAQ page. The adviser FAQ host returns 403/TLS errors and the old /faqs path is a 404.
- **Bingle** (128 Q&As): Bingle's FAQ page is rendered from an AEM GraphQL endpoint rather than static HTML. The Q&As below were extracted from that endpoint.

## Exclusions

- Health-fund booklets, policy wordings that are not PDS/SPDS, TMDs, PEDs, KFS, FSGs, forms and 'What's changed' summaries are out of scope, matching the PDS archive.
- White-label brand FAQs that duplicate an underlying insurer's content are not duplicated.
- Careers, community, sponsorship and generic news articles are excluded unless they contain product or claims Q&As.
