## Question 1: Can I remove flood cover from my AAMI Home Insurance policy to lower my premium?
**Insurer:** AAMI | **ID:** `aami-home-001` [Topics: flood, embargo, pricing]

### Official Guidance
No. Flood cover is automatically included on AAMI Home Insurance policies and cannot be removed. However, AAMI does not cover flood damage for the first 72 hours of a new policy (embargo period).

## Question 4: Do I need to add a learner driver to my policy?
**Insurer:** AAMI | **ID:** `aami-002`

### Official Guidance
Don't stress - there's no need to update your policy to include a learner driver. They're automatically covered under your insurance policy when you make a claim, as long as there's an insured, licensed driver in the front passenger seat with them when they're driving. If a learner, young or inexperienced driver is driving at the time of an incident, you may have to pay an additional excess. Check your policy documents to find out what excesses apply to your cover.Learn more about Car Insurance

## Question 11: Do I get a hire car in the event of a claim?

Enjoy the convenience of a hire car when your car's out of action following an incident covered by your policy, even if it's your fault.If you have Third Party Fire & Theft Car Insurance, and your car is stolen, we will cover the reasonable cost of a hire car that meets your transport needs for up to 21 days.Limits, exclusions and conditions apply. ## Question 12: What happens if my car is a total loss?
**Insurer:** AAMI | **ID:** `aami-010`

### Official Guidance
If you have comprehensive insurance your car becomes a total loss if:it is stolen and unrecovered after 14 days and we accept your claim for theft of your car; orit is uneconomical or unsafe to repair, including where the combined repair costs and salvage value are likely to be more than the amount covered by your policy.If your car is more than 2 years old, we pay the amount covered less any applicable deductions.For full details, please download the Comprehensive Car Insurance PDS and Additional Information Guide.If your car is less than 2 years old, and you bought it new, we'll replace it with a new car of the same make, model and series (if available within 90 days), or similar if not available. If you choose not to accept the replacement car, or where an agreement cannot be reached between us on a replacement car, or a replacement car cannot be supplied, we will pay you the amount covered on your certificate of insurance (less any deductions). See the PDS for full details.Learn more about Comprehensive Car Insurance

## Question 15: Can I drive after surgery or caesarean?
**Insurer:** AAMI | **ID:** `aami-013`

### Official Guidance
If you, or the person driving, is on medication that may impair your judgment behind the wheel, including painkillers, you may not be covered by your car insurance policy. This includes if you’re still medicated after any surgery or procedure, including a caesarean (c-section), or you’re under medical advice not to drive. If you’re in an accident post-surgery, we’ll take into account whether the surgery impaired your ability to drive when we look at your claim. We’ll also consider whether medication has affected your ability to drive.To get a better idea of what is and isn’t covered, it’s best to read your PDS.Learn more about Car Insurance

## Question 89: What is Lifetime Health Cover (LHC) loading and do I have to pay it?
**Insurer:** AAMI | **ID:** `aami-087`

### Official Guidance
Lifetime Health Cover (LHC) loading is an Australian Government initiative designed to take some burden off the public healthcare system, by encouraging people to take out private hospital cover earlier in life.If you don't take out private hospital cover by July 1, following your 31st birthday an additional cost of 2% (the loading) will be added to your hospital premium for each year you don't sign up. The loading will increase up to a maximum of 70% and will only be removed after 10 years of continuous private hospital cover. This means if you sign up at the age of 40, you'll pay an extra 20% for Hospital cover, compared to someone who joined at age 31.You can avoid paying the additional LHC by taking out AAMI hospital cover by 1 July following your 31st birthday. As long as you keep your private hospital cover, you can avoid paying the 2% annual loading. The loading only applies to the hospital component of your premiums and does not apply to Extras cover or Ambulance only cover.Learn more about hospital cover
