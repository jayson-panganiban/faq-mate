# NRMA Insurance: Official Policy & Claims FAQs

*Statutory Notice: The following FAQs reflect published disclosures by NRMA. Policy terms are subject to the applicable Product Disclosure Statement (PDS) and Target Market Determination (TMD).*

---

## Question 1: Am I covered if I accidentally leave a window or door unlocked and someone breaks in?
**Insurer:** NRMA | **ID:** `nrma-home-001` [Topics: theft, unforced-entry, burglary]

### Official Guidance
Yes. Under NRMA Home Contents Insurance, you are still covered if you accidentally leave a window or door unlocked and someone breaks into your home.

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.nrma.com.au/home-insurance](https://www.nrma.com.au/home-insurance)

## Question 2: How long does NRMA pay for temporary accommodation after an insured event?
**Insurer:** NRMA | **ID:** `nrma-home-002` [Topics: temporary-accommodation, claims, limits]

### Official Guidance
NRMA pays for reasonable temporary accommodation costs for up to 1 year under the Home Buildings and Contents Standard policy (up to 10% of sum insured), or up to 2 years under Home Buildings and Contents Plus (up to 20% of sum insured), if your home is unliveable.

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.nrma.com.au/home-insurance](https://www.nrma.com.au/home-insurance)

---

## Question 3: Does NRMA cover damage from actions of the sea or high tides?
**Insurer:** NRMA | **ID:** `nrma-home-003` [Topics: actions-of-the-sea, flood, exclusions]

### Official Guidance
No. NRMA explicitly does not cover loss or damage caused by actions of the sea, such as high tides and tidal waves. Escaped water from shower recesses or erosion is also excluded.

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.nrma.com.au/home-insurance](https://www.nrma.com.au/home-insurance)

---

## Question 4: Does NRMA guarantee the workmanship of repairs?
**Insurer:** NRMA | **ID:** `nrma-home-004` [Topics: repairs, guarantee, claims]

### Official Guidance
Yes. NRMA offers a lifetime guarantee on the workmanship of all authorized repairs completed through their network of approved partner repairers and suppliers.

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.nrma.com.au/home-insurance](https://www.nrma.com.au/home-insurance)
