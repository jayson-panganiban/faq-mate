## Question 1: What is an excess?
**Insurer:** APIA | **ID:** `apia-001`

### Official Guidance
An Excess is the amount you pay towards the cost of your claim for each incident covered by your policy. You may have to pay more than one type of excess.

The total excess you are required to pay is determined by the circumstances of your claim. The excesses that apply to your policy are shown on your certificate.

There are circumstances where your excess may be waived.

## Question 4: I've had a car accident. What do I do?
**Insurer:** APIA | **ID:** `apia-004`

### Official Guidance
Make sure everyone is safe. If it is an emergency and you or anyone else involved in the incident is injured and needs urgent medical attention, call 000.
If it is safe to do so, move your car to a safe location. If you cannot move your car and it is causing a road blockage, call the police for assistance.
Collect details of all drivers, passengers and witnesses. Make sure you have their full names, addresses and contact numbers. If another vehicle is involved, record its registration number and the driver’s insurance details. If the police attend, record the report number and name of the attending officer. Do not admit fault to anyone.
Contact us as soon as possible. Make sure you have the details of the incident including date, time and location at hand to assist us with lodging your claim. If towing is required, we will help arrange the towing of your car to a location we nominate for assessment.

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.apia.com.au/faqs/car/accident.html#accident](https://www.apia.com.au/faqs/car/accident.html#accident)

## Question 8: Does Apia offer cover notes?
**Insurer:** APIA | **ID:** `apia-008`

### Official Guidance
No, Apia does not offer cover notes (a temporary insurance that provides cover between signing up for a new policy and making the initial premium payment). When you commence cover with an Apia Car Insurance policy, you can pay through a number of convenient options, including online, via BPAY, in person, and by mail. Discover our full list of payment method options.
Apia also offers a 21-day cooling off period on our Comprehensive Car Insurance.
This means that when you sign up for a new Comprehensive Car Insurance policy, you’ll have 21 days to cancel your policy, if you choose to, and get a full refund (as long as you haven’t made any claims in that time).

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.apia.com.au/faqs/car/Apia-offer-cover-notes.html#Apia-offer-cover-notes](https://www.apia.com.au/faqs/car/Apia-offer-cover-notes.html#Apia-offer-cover-notes)

## Question 9: What happens if I miss my monthly instalment payment?
**Insurer:** APIA | **ID:** `apia-009`

### Official Guidance
If you miss a monthly instalment you can make a one off payment via your Apia Online Services online account.

To make a one off payment, follow the below steps: Log into Apia Online Services; Select the “Policies” button; Select the relevant policy from your policy list  
If you’re eligible to make a manual payment, click the “Pay now” button which will appear at the top of the Policy Summary screen. If you’re not able to make a payment the box will not appear.
You will be able check and update your direct debit or card details with us following your successful payment. You can also update your payment details when you log into Apia Online Services at any time, accessible via the Policy Summary screen.
If a monthly instalment is overdue we will let you know. We can cancel your policy by giving you at least 14 days advance notice, but if a monthly instalment is overdue by a month, we can cancel your policy without advance notice.

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.apia.com.au/faqs/payments/what-happens-if-i-miss-my-monthly-instalment-payment.html#what-happens-if-i-miss-my-monthly-instalment-payment](https://www.apia.com.au/faqs/payments/what-happens-if-i-miss-my-monthly-instalment-payment.html#what-happens-if-i-miss-my-monthly-instalment-payment)
