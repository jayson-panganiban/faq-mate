# LUMLEY SPECIAL VEHICLES Insurance: Official Policy & Claims FAQs

*Statutory Notice: The following FAQs reflect published disclosures by LUMLEY SPECIAL VEHICLES. Policy terms are subject to the applicable Product Disclosure Statement (PDS) and Target Market Determination (TMD).*

---

## Question 1: What sort of insurance do you provide?
**Insurer:** LUMLEY SPECIAL VEHICLES | **ID:** `lumley-special-vehicles-001`

### Official Guidance
LSV offers Comprehensive Insurance covering loss or damage to your car, as well as damage you may cause to someone else's vehicle or property, up to $30,000,000. They also offer Third Party Property Damage Insurance, which protects against claims for damage you may cause to another person's car or property, also up to $30,000,000.

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.lsvcarinsurance.com.au/help-and-support?](https://www.lsvcarinsurance.com.au/help-and-support?)

## Question 4: What is an agreed value?
**Insurer:** LUMLEY SPECIAL VEHICLES | **ID:** `lumley-special-vehicles-004`

### Official Guidance
The agreed value is the maximum amount LSV will pay if your vehicle is damaged or stolen. This value is set at the start of your policy and is based on your car's make, model, accessories, and modifications that LSV has agreed to cover. The agreed value remains constant during the coverage period and is displayed on your policy schedule.

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.lsvcarinsurance.com.au/help-and-support?](https://www.lsvcarinsurance.com.au/help-and-support?)

## Question 5: How do I make a claim?
**Insurer:** LUMLEY SPECIAL VEHICLES | **ID:** `lumley-special-vehicles-005`

### Official Guidance
If your car has been damaged, stolen, or someone is claiming against you, call LSV on 133 578. If your car has been stolen or maliciously damaged, it's important to report the incident to the police immediately and obtain the report or event number.

## Question 6: Can I use my own repairer?
**Insurer:** LUMLEY SPECIAL VEHICLES | **ID:** `lumley-special-vehicles-006`

### Official Guidance
Yes, if LSV elects to repair your car, you may choose your own licensed repairer. LSV guarantees the workmanship and materials on all repairs they authorize for the life of the vehicle.

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.lsvcarinsurance.com.au/help-and-support?](https://www.lsvcarinsurance.com.au/help-and-support?)

## Question 7: Can I get my car back if it is declared a write-off?
**Insurer:** LUMLEY SPECIAL VEHICLES | **ID:** `lumley-special-vehicles-007`

### Official Guidance
If your car is 25 years old or more and LSV declares it a total loss, you may keep the salvage of your car at no extra cost. For cars less than 25 years old, you may choose to purchase the salvage at a fair and reasonable cost set by LSV.

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.lsvcarinsurance.com.au/help-and-support?](https://www.lsvcarinsurance.com.au/help-and-support?)

## Question 9: Are learner drivers covered under my policy?
**Insurer:** LUMLEY SPECIAL VEHICLES | **ID:** `lumley-special-vehicles-009`

### Official Guidance
Learner drivers are covered under the policy as long as they drive under the instruction of a fully licensed driver alongside them.
