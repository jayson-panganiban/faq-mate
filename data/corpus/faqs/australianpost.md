## Question 2: What does Comprehensive Car Insurance cover?
**Insurer:** AUSTRALIANPOST | **ID:** `australianpost-002`

### Official Guidance
We offer three types of car insurance products – Comprehensive, Third Party and Compulsory Third Party (also known as CTP or Green Slip. Note that CTP is only available in New South Wales).
Of these products, Comprehensive Car Insurance offers the broadest level of protection. It covers repairs to your car for damage caused by things like collision, fire, theft and weather events. It also covers damage to someone else’s vehicle or property caused by the use of your car.
For more information please read the Combined Financial Services Guide and Product Disclosure Statement.

## Question 3: What should I do if I have a car accident?
**Insurer:** AUSTRALIANPOST | **ID:** `australianpost-003`

### Official Guidance
If you are covered by an Australia Post car insurance policy and you have a car accident, we're here to help. Check that all passengers at the accident scene are safe. If anyone is injured or there is significant property damage, call the police immediately, and an ambulance if needed. If there are any other drivers involved, ask to see their driver licence and record their:  name, address , phone numbers , registration number, car insurance details.   
Make sure you also write down the contact details of any witnesses. Have this information handy when you contact the claims team. You can call them 24 hours a day on 13 70 11.

## Question 4: What’s the difference between agreed and market value of a car?
**Insurer:** AUSTRALIANPOST | **ID:** `australianpost-004`

### Official Guidance
Agreed value is the amount you and your insurer agree to insure your car for, for one period of insurance. You can find the agreed value on your Certificate of Insurance.  
Market value is the amount your vehicle is worth on the open market right before the claimed incident. As part of the claims process, QBE (our insurance provider) will determine the market value of your car using recognised industry guides. Factors like its make, model, age, kilometres travelled, accessories and modifications fitted as well as its general condition will also contribute to determining the value of your car.  
Your choice of agreed value or market value is reflected in your premium.

## Question 5: What’s the difference between CTP and comprehensive insurance?
**Insurer:** AUSTRALIANPOST | **ID:** `australianpost-005`

### Official Guidance
Compulsory third party insurance, also known as a CTP or Green Slip, is mandatory in Australia. In NSW, QLD, SA and ACT you may choose your CTP provider. (Similar personal injury insurance is included in a motor vehicle’s registration cost in other parts of Australia.)
If you or anyone driving your car causes an accident in which someone else is injured, CTP covers their medical costs and compensation they are entitled to.
CTP doesn’t cover damage to your car or the responsibility of the driver of your car for damage caused to other people’s vehicles or property.

## Question 6: What’s the difference between comprehensive and third party car insurance?
**Insurer:** AUSTRALIANPOST | **ID:** `australianpost-006`

### Official Guidance
Third party car insurance offers an essential level of protection. It provides cover for damage caused to someone else’s vehicle or property which was caused by the use of your car. For an extra premium, our Third Party Car Insurance can also cover your car for fire and theft, for up to $10,000.  
Comprehensive car insurance offers an additional level of protection. Australia Post Comprehensive Car Insurance is our broadest level of cover. It covers repairs to your car for damage caused by things like collision, fire, theft and weather events. It also covers damage to someone else’s vehicle or property caused by the use of your car.
For more information about the benefits of the different types of car insurance, please read the relevant Combined Financial Services Guide and Product Disclosure Statement
