# Allianz Comprehensive Motor Insurance (PDS V12B)

*Jurisdiction: Australia (Cth) | Effective Date: 2024-03-01*

---

## Section 1: Standard Defined Events & Operative Coverage

### Insured Event 1: Accidental Collision & Vehicle Damage
#### Coverage Scope
We cover accidental loss of or physical damage to your insured Vehicle caused by collision, overturning, fire, explosion, lightning, hail, storm, or flood occurring during the period of insurance within Australia.

#### New Vehicle Replacement Benefit
If your vehicle is less than 24 months old from original registration and becomes a total loss, we will replace it with a new vehicle of the same make, model, and specification, subject to availability.

---

### Insured Event 2: Theft and Attempted Theft
#### Coverage Scope
We cover theft or illegal conversion of your vehicle or parts attached to it. 

#### Mandatory Additional Benefit: Hire Car Following Theft
If your vehicle is stolen and the theft is accepted under this policy:
- We provide a compact replacement hire car up to **$100 per day** for a maximum of **21 days**, or until your vehicle is recovered undamaged or the claim is settled.
- You must report the theft to the Police immediately and obtain a police incident report number.

---

## Section 2: Critical Exclusions (WHAT IS NOT COVERED)

### Exclusion 1: Unlicensed Driver Exclusion
We do not cover any accidental loss, damage, or third-party legal liability if, at the time of the incident, your vehicle is being driven by, or in the control of, any person who:
1. Does not hold a valid, current Australian driver licence required by law for that class of vehicle;
2. Is driving in breach of the conditions of their licence (including provisional, learner, or probationary restrictions); or
3. Has had their driver licence suspended, cancelled, disqualified, or revoked;

**Owner Consent Condition:** This exclusion applies whenever you or an authorised person gave permission or consented to that person driving, or where you knew or ought reasonably to have known that the driver was unlicensed.

---

### Exclusion 2: Alcohol, Drugs and Intoxication
We do not cover loss, damage, or liability if the driver of your vehicle:
- Has a Blood Alcohol Concentration (BAC) exceeding the statutory legal limit prescribed in the state or territory of occurrence; or
- Is under the influence of any drug or intoxicating substance; or
- Refuses to submit to a lawful breath, blood, or urine test required by law enforcement.

---

### Exclusion 3: Unroadworthy or Damaged Condition
We do not cover loss, damage, or liability if your vehicle is being driven in an unroadworthy, unsafe, or overloaded condition where such condition contributed to the accident, and you knew or ought reasonably to have known of the unroadworthy condition.

---

### Exclusion 4: Commercial Ride-Sharing & Courier Use
We do not cover loss or damage if the vehicle is being used for hire, fare, reward, delivery services, or peer-to-peer ride sharing (e.g. Uber, DiDi, DoorDash) unless you have declared this commercial usage to us and it is specifically endorsed on your Policy Schedule.

## Section 4: Excess Schedules & Deductibles

An excess is the amount you contribute towards each claim:

| Excess Type | Standard Amount | Applicable Conditions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Basic Policy Excess** | As stated on Policy Schedule (default $850) | Applies to all claims unless non-fault |
| **Age Excess (Driver aged under 21)** | $600 additional excess | Applies on top of Basic Excess |
| **Age Excess (Driver aged 21 to 24)** | $500 additional excess | Applies on top of Basic Excess |
| **Inexperienced Driver Excess** | $400 additional excess | Driver aged 25+ licensed <2 years |
| **Undisclosed / Unlisted Driver Excess** | $1,000 additional excess | Driver not named on certificate |
