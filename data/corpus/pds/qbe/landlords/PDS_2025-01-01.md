Condition of your building or home unit What we won’t cover There is no cover under any section of your Policy for any claim, loss, cost, damage, injury, death or legal liability caused by or arising from:
- your failure to fix:
a defect;
a structural fault;
a design fault; or faulty workmanship as soon as is reasonable after you become aware of it, or a reasonable person in the circumstances would have become aware of it;
