Bilateral conditions A bilateral condition is any condition that can happen on both sides of your pet’s body. Examples of bilateral conditions include:
- conditions affecting eyes, including cataracts, cherry eye, ectropion, and entropion
- conditions affecting legs, including hip dysplasia, patella luxation, and cruciate ligament conditions.
If your pet has a condition on one side of the body, the risk of the same condition occurring on the other side of the body increases, whether at the same time or later.
We consider any bilateral condition as a single condition.
- If we cover a bilateral condition on one side of your pet’s body, we will cover the condition if it occurs on the other side.
- If your pet has a pre-existing bilateral condition, we will not cover the condition if it occurs on the other side.
For example, if your pet’s left knee has a pre-existing cruciate ligament condition, we will not cover that condition in either knee.
