Stand-alone insurances are held independently of each other and when a benefit is paid under one of the insurances, it does not reduce the benefit for any other insurance held.
If benefits are linked, the payment of a claim under one linked benefit reduces the sum insured under the other linked benefits.
This leads to a lower premium for linked insurances than for non-linked Insurances.
