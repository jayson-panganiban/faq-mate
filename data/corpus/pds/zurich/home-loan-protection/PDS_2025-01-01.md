### Definitions — Flood
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

Under **Zurich Home Loan Protection** (PDS) and the *Insurance Contracts Regulations 2012 (Cth)* Reg 29D standard definition, **Flood** means the covering of normally dry land by water that has escaped or been released from the normal confines of any lake, river, creek, or other natural watercourse (whether or not altered or modified), or any reservoir, canal, or dam.

**Related Water Peril Demarcation:**
- **Storm & Rainwater Run-off:** Surface water or rainwater run-off flowing over normally dry ground during a storm without escaping from a natural watercourse or dam is assessed under **Insured Event 3 (Storm cover)** rather than Flood.
- **Escape of Liquid:** Sudden bursting or leaking from fixed internal pipes, appliances, or hot water systems is assessed under **Insured Event 4 (Escape of Liquid)**.
- **Certificate of Insurance:** Your *Certificate of Insurance* (Policy Schedule) specifies whether Flood cover is included or opted out, along with any applicable Flood or Storm Excess.
- **Official Source URL:** [https://www.zurich.com.au/-/media-assets/project/zurich-headless/shared/content/dam/au-documents/personal/hlp/zurich-home-loan-protection-pds.pdf](https://www.zurich.com.au/-/media-assets/project/zurich-headless/shared/content/dam/au-documents/personal/hlp/zurich-home-loan-protection-pds.pdf)

#### CRITICAL POLICY EXCLUSIONS & LIMITATIONS (WHAT IS NOT COVERED):
- Actions of the Sea & Storm Surge: Loss or damage caused by high tide, king tides, tidal waves, or actions of the sea is excluded.
- 72-Hour Waiting Period (Embargo): No cover applies for flood, storm, bushfire, or cyclone during the first 72 hours of a new policy unless replacing equivalent cover without lapse.
- Retaining walls, sea walls, freestanding outdoor gates/fences, and gradual seepage/hydrostatic pressure are excluded.

> ⚠️ **Mandatory Embargo Window:** A strict **72-hour embargo** applies post-purchase. No cover for losses occurring within 72 hours of policy inception unless transferring without lapse.

When your employment changes You need to tell us if, after your policy start date, you resign from your current occupation or start working less than 20 hours a week as you may no longer be eligible to receive this benefit, and your cover may need to be reduced accordingly.
Qualifying and waiting period for cover Qualifying period (applies to illness only) You are not covered for any illness:
- arising from a medical condition that you are diagnosed with in the first 30 days after the policy starts, or
- where the symptoms leading to the illness are reasonably apparent in the first 30 days after the policy starts.
Waiting period (applies to illness only) For each claim after the event date, a 30-day waiting period applies. This waiting period starts on the day a medical practitioner confirms that you are unable to work due to an illness. You must continue paying your premiums during the waiting period.
