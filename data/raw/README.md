# Australian Insurer PDS / SPDS Archive

A working archive of **Product Disclosure Statements (PDS)** and **Supplementary Product Disclosure Statements (SPDS)** for Australian insurance products, organised by insurer and product.

- **1,277 PDFs**, ~1,230 MB
- **39 insurer folders**, each with its own `README.md` and `manifest.csv`
- Every PDF validated with `pdfinfo` — **zero corrupt or zero-page files**
- Machine-readable index: [`all_manifest.csv`](all_manifest.csv) (insurer, category, product, type, title, file, bytes, URL)
- Per-insurer summary: [`archive_summary.csv`](archive_summary.csv)

## What is included

- **PDS** and **SPDS** documents published on each insurer's product-disclosure page.
- **Combined PDS and policy documents** where the insurer publishes the PDS and policy terms as one document.
- **Combined PDS and FSG** documents where that combined document is the only PDS the insurer publishes.
- **PDS-equivalent policy wordings** only where the insurer does not publish a separate PDS (three documents, marked as such in `all_manifest.csv`).
- **Historical and superseded editions** wherever the insurer still links them from its site. This is why several folders are much deeper than the current product range — for example Zurich (2003–2026), AIA (2009–2026), NobleOak (2009–2024) and Suncorp.
- **Current editions** for every product range that publishes a public PDS.

## What is excluded

- Target Market Determinations (TMD), Key Fact Sheets (KFS), Premium Excess and Discount Guides (PED) and Additional Information Guides (AIG).
- Standalone Financial Services Guides (FSG) — except where the FSG is combined with the PDS in the one document.
- PDS updates, significant event notices, addenda and policy-only documents that are not part of a combined PDS.
- Application forms, claim forms, brochures, annual reports, constitutions and governance documents.
- Health-fund policy booklets and fact sheets (for example the AAMI and APIA health pages), which are not PDS documents.
- Investment and superannuation PDS where the issuer is not an insurer (for example the BT platform, Australian Unity Lifeplan investment bonds and the Challenger Guaranteed Income Fund).
- White-label brand PDS (Coles, Woolworths, Kmart, Bupa, NIB, bank travel and similar). Those documents are issued by the underlying insurer and duplicate the PDS in the relevant insurer folder; the underlying insurer's current and historical PDS are archived here.

## How it was built

1. Each insurer's public policy-document page was fetched and parsed for PDF links.
2. Links were filtered to PDS/SPDS (plus combined PDS/policy and PDS-equivalent wordings), de-duplicated by URL, and grouped into product folders.
3. PDFs were downloaded with a browser user-agent and a per-insurer referer, validated by the `%PDF` header, and retried up to three times.
4. Sites that block plain HTTP clients (Budget Direct's secure host, RAC's NobleOak host, NobleOak, and some CDN links) were fetched through a real browser and saved through a local upload server.
5. Every file was verified with `pdfinfo`; the final audit compares the manifest against the files on disk (no orphans, no missing files).

## Known gaps and dead links

- **1Cover** — the 19 December 2024 Standard PDS link is a dead 404. The current edition and the other historical editions are present.
- **RACT** — the May 2023 travel PDS link is a dead 404. The other travel editions are present.
- **Vero** — the 27 October 2022 retail PDS link is a dead 404. The other Vero editions are present.
- **Integrity Life** — the business was transferred to AIA; there is no standalone public PDS site. AIA's archive is included.
- **Australian Unity** — no longer publishes a life-risk PDS; its general insurance is issued by Allianz and its investment bonds are not insurance PDS. Not archived as a life insurer.
- **BT** — a wealth and superannuation platform rather than a life insurer; excluded.
- **HCF Life** — `hcflife.com.au` timed out; the PDS documents were taken from `hcf.com.au/lifeinfo`.

## Re-running

Each insurer folder's `README.md` shows the source page and the command to re-run that batch. The combined batches are:

```bash
cd "Default Project"
python3 more_download.py suncorp_links.json suncorp_manifest.csv
python3 more_download.py more_links.json more_manifest.csv
python3 more_download.py more2_links.json more2_manifest.csv
python3 more_download.py travel_links.json travel_manifest.csv
python3 more_download.py life_links.json life_manifest.csv
```

`more_download.py` keeps existing valid files (cached), so re-running only fetches missing or failed documents. `_work/make_all_manifest.py` rebuilds `all_manifest.csv`; `_work/make_root_readme.py` rebuilds this README and `archive_summary.csv`.
