## Section: General

### What is a Medical Only travel insurance?

Our International Medical Only policy is a lower-cost alternative international travel insurance that provides cover for emergency medical treatment, accidental injury or unforeseen illness. It does not include other benefits usually found in a comprehensive policy such as trip cancellations or delays and lost or stolen luggage and personal effects.

### What happens once I've submitted my claim?

Once you submit a claim, we will send you an email to acknowledge we received it. This email will also let you know how long it will be before we aim to have a claims assessor contact you. We need you to complete the Declaration and Privacy Act form. A link to the form will be included in your email. Although you’ve already provided authority to us, the majority of third parties will require this form prior to releasing information to us. Completing the form and sending it to us immediately can reduce any delays. If we require further information from you and/or a third party (such as your doctor, travel agent or the police),your claims assessor will keep you updated with the progress and about what information is required. When we have made a decision about your claim, your claim assessor will confirm the outcome to you. We’ll always confirm the decision in writing, but may call you to discuss it first.

### How do I make a claim?

Click ‘Make a claim’. Log in with your policy number and email used when purchasing the policy. A 6-digit verification code will be sent to your email address. Once you have entered the verification code, click ‘Log In’. To make your claim, click ‘Initiate a New Claim’. Fill out the required details of the claim event and select the reasons for your claim from the displayed benefit options. Click ‘Next’. Follow through with the journey and enter all your claimed expenses individually. Attach relevant supporting documentation and upload. Click ‘Next’. Review your information and if it is all correct, click Next. Add your bank details and read both the Declaration and Privacy Act authorisation. If you agree, check each box and click ‘Submit’. You can also submit a claim by clicking on ‘Manage Claims’ in the blue bar at the top of your My SCTI dashboard and expand the side panel to find ‘Make a Claim’.

### What should I do if I need to send additional documents for my claim?

You can email the additional receipts and supporting documents to us at claims@scti.com.au. Please make sure that you include your claim number in the subject line of your email – this will ensure there are no delays in adding your documents to your claim. It’s best to retain the original documents, as we may need them from you at a later date. If you don’t have a scanner for your documents, then we’ll be happy to accept clear photos of the documents from your mobile. Please ensure the document can be clearly read before emailing it to us. If you choose to post your documents to us, we recommend sending clear, readable copies of original documents and not the original item, to prevent original items being lost in the post. ### What do I need to provide if I'm claiming for lost, stolen or damaged items?

When claiming for any lost, stolen or damaged items, it's important to send us as much documentation as possible to support your claim. Receipts help to prove ownership and value – that you owned the item, and what the item was worth at the time it was purchased. Sometimes it is quite tough to supply receipts for everything you own, especially in the case of an entire suitcase going missing! For unspecified items, if you don't have receipts, we can consider bank statements, valuations, or reprinted receipts from the store you purchased the item/s from. If you don't have any of these things, then please discuss this with the claims assessor who is handling your claim. For specified items, the policy wording requires that for items less than 12 months old at the time of loss, you provide the original receipt, and for items more than 12 months old at the time of loss, you provide the original receipt and a current valuation that is no more than 24 months old at the time when you took out the cover for the item. We'll also need confirmation of the event. For example, if you were pick pocketed while walking along a busy street, make sure you speak about the incident to the police or other relevant authority and obtain a written report from them. For example, if your luggage goes missing while stored with a service provider, obtain a report from the lost luggage department. Make sure you send us the reports. If you're claiming for damaged items, we'll need to see a report from a suitable expert or outlet whether the damage is repairable, and a quote for the repair costs. Please do not have the item repaired, and retain the item in case we ask you to send it to us. Also, please send us a copy of your itinerary, boarding passes, or something that can confirm you were overseas at the time of the incident. Each traveller’s situation is different from the next, so please do feel free to contact us to clarify any questions you may have about your particular incident.

### Why can't I get the travel insurance cover I need?

If we cannot provide you with insurance cover, it’s likely to be for one of the following reasons: some destinations present greater risk than others, or the Government may have raised travel advisories, so we have decided not to provide cover; you don’t meet other requirements of our eligibility criteria; or some pre-existing medical conditions present greater risk than others, and we cannot therefore offer you cover for your particular pre-existing medical conditions. You have the right to ask us for the information we relied on to make the decision not to provide cover. For information about your options for alternative insurance cover, or approaching another insurer or an insurance broker, please contact either the Insurance Council of Australia or the National Insurance Brokers Association of Australia. If you are unhappy with our decision, you can find information about our complaints management process here.

### When I purchase a policy, how do travel warnings affect my cover?

You must make sure it’s still safe to travel to your destinations by checking for travel advisories on the Smartraveller website. Your policy may be affected if the travel advisory on the Smartraveller website is ‘Reconsider your need to travel’ or ‘Do not travel’. You need to check this when you buy your insurance, again before you start your journey, and before leaving for each new destination. The table below shows how travel advisories affect your policy. Type of travel advisory Effect on your policy A travel advisory that affects just part of a country. You won’t be covered for events in that part of the country that relate to that travel advisory. A travel advisory that affects the whole country You won’t be covered for events anywhere in that country that relate to that travel advisory. To understand how the travel warnings apply to the areas of a country, it’s important to visit the Smartraveller website before you purchase your insurance.

### How do travel warnings affect whether I can buy a policy?

When a country has a ‘Reconsider your need to travel’ or ‘Do not travel’ warning, we may decide to either: sell you a policy, and these will be highlighted on our website; or not sell you a policy – these destinations cannot be selected on our website. If you cannot select a country, it may mean we have removed the destination from our website due to the extreme risk that the destination poses to travellers.

### What is a travel warning?

The Australian Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade (DFAT) issues travel advisories for destinations. These are published on the Smartraveller website. When the travel advisory is at a ‘Reconsider your need to travel’ (level 3) or ‘Do not travel’ (level 4) level, we call this a ‘travel warning’. It is important that you understand how these travel warnings affect your cover, before you purchase your insurance. In addition to any DFAT travel warning, it is important to understand what border controls (including visa , insurance, or vaccine requirements) may be in place for the destination(s) you are travelling to. This information is generally available through the immigration department of the applicable country. There is no provision for cover if you do not have the correct visa, visa waiver, vaccinations, or documents needed for your journey.

### What do I need to provide if I'm claiming for medical expenses?

If you need to make a claim for medical expenses, here are some documents that we may require when assessing your claim: Receipts for your medical expenses, together with any consultation notes or reports provided by the treating doctor. If your claim is related to costs to see a medical specialist, please send us your referral letter. This is the letter your GP would have sent to the specialist. If you have been admitted to hospital or have incurred ambulance costs, please send us a full admission and discharge summary, or any other reports the hospital gave you. Please keep a copy of your documents and receipts for your own records. We may ask for them during your claims assessment Sometimes we may need to obtain information not described above, and we will notify you if this is the case. If you don’t provide the information required, it may result in a delay with the processing of your claim.
