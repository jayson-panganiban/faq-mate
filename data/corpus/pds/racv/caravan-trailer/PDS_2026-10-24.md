**Clause Classification:** `SubLimit`

Your current Certificate of Insurance shows the amount and types of excesses that apply to your policy.

deduct the excess from any claim payment, or request you pay the excess to us or, to the repairer or supplier.

When an excess applies If you make any type of claim you must pay any basic excess that applies.
