## Insurance

### Do I get a hire car after an insured event occurs?

You can add a hire car as an optional extra for an additional premium to your RACQ Comprehensive motor insurance policy. After an insured event you can get a hire car for up to 21 days, at (up to) $75 a day if you have added this optional extra to your policy. If you are a Gold50 RACQ member who holds Roadside Assistance, you will receive this benefit at no extra cost.

### What time period does my pet illness insurance cover?

Your cover is for the period of insurance shown on your Certificate of Insurance and is only available after any applicable waiting period has passed.

You can also claim vet costs for an illness that we covered under one period of insurance and which continues to require treatment in the following period of insurance. Cover for this continuing treatment will be provided as long as we continue to offer this pet insurance product and you renew your policy each year with no gap or break in cover.

*Source: <https://www.racq.com.au/help-contact/insurance/how-does-pet-illness-cover-work>*

### What doesn't my pet illness insurance cover?

Your pet illness insurance doesn't cover:

- any illness caused by your dog not being adequately vaccinated against:
- parvovirus,
- adenovirus (infectious canine hepatitis)
- distemper
- bordetella bronchiseptica (kennel cough)
- parainfluenza virus (kennel cough)
- any illness caused by your cat not being adequately vaccinated against:
- feline herpesvirus (viral rhinotracheitis)
- calicivirus
- panleukopenia (feline enteritis)
- chlamydophila
- feline immunodeficiency virus (FIV)
- feline leukaemia virus (FeLV)
- any illness that your pet suffers during a waiting period
- any pre-existing conditions

Read our [Product Disclosure Statement (PDS), any applicable Supplementary PDS and Target Market Determinations](https://www.racq.com.au/insurance/insurance-disclosure-documents) for more information.

There are some things your policy doesn’t cover, including:

- Accessories
- After hours treatment and house calls
- Alternative therapy
- Boarding and transporting
- Breeding
- Commercial or occupational purposes
- Damage caused by your pet
- Dangerous dogs
- Deliberate acts
- Dental care, except where treatment is required because of an accidental injury to your pet's teeth such as a fractured tooth due to trauma.
- Elective treatments

*Source: <https://www.racq.com.au/help-contact/insurance/how-does-pet-illness-cover-work>*

### How do I know what countries are included in each region?

When you select a region of travel, the insurer covers all the countries included within that region, but will not cover countries and areas of travel which are the subject of a “Do Not Travel“ advisory issued by DFAT (check [www.smartraveller.gov.au/](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/)) prior to you purchasing your policy or before you travel. For simplicity, your Certificate of Insurance lists the region name rather than each country individually.

To help you understand exactly where you're covered, we've provided a detailed list of countries grouped by region.

### How does the Emergency Boarding benefit work?

This RACQ Pet Insurance benefit covers the costs to board your pet at a licensed cattery or kennel if you are unexpectedly admitted to hospital for 5 or more consecutive days, up to an annual limit of $1,000.

*Source: <https://www.racq.com.au/help-contact/insurance/how-does-the-emergency-boarding-benefit-work>*

### What doesn’t the Emergency Boarding benefit cover?

This benefit doesn’t include cover for circumstances where:

- You’re in hospital to have an elective surgery, or because of something that happened, or you knew about, before the policy started.
- Someone who lives with you is able to look after your pet while you are in hospital.

*Source: <https://www.racq.com.au/help-contact/insurance/how-does-the-emergency-boarding-benefit-work>*

### Which Medical Conditions are automatically covered in my travel insurance?

Specified Medical Conditions are automatically included, where you, at the Relevant Time:

1. have not required hospitalisation or treatment (where treatment does not include an annual or routine medical check-up, blood testing or a visit to a medical practitioner to obtain a regular prescription) by any Treating Doctor within the last 24 months (unless a different time-period is specifically listed in the list below) for the Medical Condition;
2. are not awaiting the outcome of any investigation, tests, surgery or other treatment for the Medical Condition; and
3. meet any additional criteria set out in the Medical Conditions we automatically cover as listed in the [PDS](https://www.racq.com.au/insurance/insurance-disclosure-documents#travel).

If the criteria above are satisfied, cover is automatically included for the Medical Conditions listed in the [PDS](https://www.racq.com.au/insurance/insurance-disclosure-documents#travel).

*Source: <https://www.racq.com.au/help-contact/insurance/which-medical-conditions-are-automatically-covered-in-my-travel-insurance>*

### Which travel insurance policy do I select if I am going on a cruise?

Cover is provided for river cruising and ocean cruising regardless of whether you are cruising overseas or within Australia. A number of options are available subject to the terms, conditions and exclusions in the policy.5

The definition of Cruise means an ocean voyage on a commercially operated vessel for more than one night. If you are on a cruise for only one night or you are on a river cruise, you will automatically be covered under the policy without selecting the Cruise Option.

The policy cannot cover any medical expenses within Australia, so if your cruise might be in Australian waters, please check carefully if there are medical facilities onboard, and if so, whether Medicare applies.

## Support for financial hardship: Insurance claims

### Am I eligible to claim financial hardship?

Financial hardship can be caused by many factors - such as losing your job, reduced income, or an unexpected and costly emergency. If you’re experiencing difficulties making payments to RACQ relating to an insurance claim for the collection of an excess or a debt recovery, then you can apply for financial hardship assistance.

*Source: <https://www.racq.com.au/help-contact/insurance-claims-hardship>*

### I’m experiencing financial hardship and need help with my other RACQ products, can you help?

If you’re struggling to make payments for loans, insurance premiums or roadside assistance products held through RACQ, our team can help you. For more information about how we can help you, visit our website [here](https://www.racq.com.au/about-us/group-member-advocate/supporting-vulnerability).

*Source: <https://www.racq.com.au/help-contact/insurance-claims-hardship>*

### I've submitted my request for help, what happens now?

For more information about this process, read our financial hardship [customer information fact sheet](https://www.racq.com.au/-/media/project/racqgroup/racq/pdf/help-contact/claims-financial-hardship-customer-information-sheet-v12.pdf?rev=3966849393164f62975463c73243489c&hash=0AC52A392727FF63CF83583B0D17E258).

1. We will assess your financial hardship application and work with you to provide support that is tailored to your needs.
2. We may request further information or supporting documentation from you. We will only do so when it is relevant to support your application and help us make the right decision for you. We will outline specifically what we require from you to complete the assessment of your application at the earliest time we can.
3. We will communicate to you in writing of our decision within 21 calendar days of receiving all the information we require for an assessment to be made.

*Source: <https://www.racq.com.au/help-contact/insurance-claims-hardship>*

### What is a roof upgrade?

- Replacing deteriorated or split battens
- Replacing nails with screws or straps in batten-to-rafter/ truss connections under sheet metal roofs
- Replacing skew nails with straps or bolts in rafter/truss-to-wall connections
- Installation of structural tie-down to the roof, which can include an over-batten roof system (see below)
