# QBE Insurance: Official Policy & Claims FAQs

*Statutory Notice: The following FAQs reflect published disclosures by QBE. Policy terms are subject to the applicable Product Disclosure Statement (PDS) and Target Market Determination (TMD).*

## Question 1: What is the QBE Buildings Sum Insured Safeguard benefit?
**Insurer:** QBE | **ID:** `qbe-home-001` [Topics: sum-insured, safeguard, rebuilding]

### Official Guidance
QBE's Buildings Sum Insured Safeguard may pay up to an additional 30% above your nominated buildings sum insured to cover unexpected increases in labor and rebuilding costs due to a surge after a major catastrophe.

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.qbe.com/au/home-insurance](https://www.qbe.com/au/home-insurance)

## Question 2: What is the legal liability limit on QBE Home Insurance?
**Insurer:** QBE | **ID:** `qbe-home-002` [Topics: liability, sub-limits]

### Official Guidance
QBE provides up to $30 million in legal liability cover worldwide if you are found legally responsible for accidental injury to someone or damage to third-party property.

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.qbe.com/au/home-insurance](https://www.qbe.com/au/home-insurance)

## Question 3: Are my contents covered while moving between my old and new home?
**Insurer:** QBE | **ID:** `qbe-home-003` [Topics: moving, contents, transit]

### Official Guidance
Yes. QBE covers your contents for up to 60 days across both your old and new residences while you are moving.
