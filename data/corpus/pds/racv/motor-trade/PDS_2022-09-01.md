(16) Electronic Communication (a) It may be possible to have your policy documents sent to you electronically by e-mail. If you tell us to send your policy documents to you electronically, then we will send them to the e-mail address you give us rather than to your mailing address.
(b) Any policy documents we send to your e-mail address are considered to have been received by you 24 hours from when we send them. If you don’t tell us to send your policy documents to you electronically, then we will send them by post to the mailing address you gave us.
(c) You are responsible for making sure the e-mail and mailing address we have for you are up to date.
