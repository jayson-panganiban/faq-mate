## Question 1: How does Suncorp define a flood under the Insurance Contracts Regulations?
**Insurer:** SUNCORP | **ID:** `suncorp-home-001` [Topics: flood, prescribed-definition, embargo]

### Official Guidance
Under the Insurance Contracts Regulations 2012 (Cth), Suncorp defines flood as the covering of normally dry land by water that has escaped or been released from the normal confines of any natural watercourse, lake, reservoir, canal, or dam. Suncorp policies exclude flood damage occurring in the first 72 hours of policy purchase.

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.suncorp.com.au/insurance/home-contents.html](https://www.suncorp.com.au/insurance/home-contents.html)

## Question 2: What is Suncorp's standard sub-limit for jewelry and watches?
**Insurer:** SUNCORP | **ID:** `suncorp-home-002` [Topics: jewelry, sub-limits, contents]

### Official Guidance
Under Suncorp Classic Home Contents, jewelry and watches have a standard policy sub-limit of $2,500 total, with a maximum of $1,000 for any single item, unless individually listed on the policy certificate.

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.suncorp.com.au/insurance/home-contents.html](https://www.suncorp.com.au/insurance/home-contents.html)
