### Gold Car - Low Kilometres PDS (Part 2)
**Clause Classification:** `Coverage`

An Additional Excess will apply if the car exceeds the agreed number of 'Kilometres Per Year', if you do not provide us with an odometer reading within the required timeframe, or provide us with an invalid odometer reading, such as one that is higher or lower than the actual reading at the time. We use the car's odometer reading at the time of incident and the odometer reading as shown on your most recent Insurance Certificate to determine how far the car has travelled.
If you believe the agreed number of 'Kilometres Per Year' is likely to be exceeded, you will need to contact us to update your policy. Increasing the 'Kilometres Per Year' may result in an additional premium being applied, or you no longer being eligible for a Low Kilometres Policy.

### Excess On Claims
**Clause Classification:** `SubLimit`

The following excesses may apply to your claim and will be shown on your Insurance Certificate:
l Basic Excess applies to all claims.
l Additional Excesses apply when the car is driven by a person who is not an excluded driver, but who:
- is not listed as a driver on the Insurance Certificate, and/or
- is under 25 years of age, and/or
- has not held a full or open Australian licence for 2 or more years.
l Additional Excess also applies when the car is being driven and:
- at the time of an incident, it has exceeded the agreed 'Kilometres Per Year',
- you did not provide the odometer reading of the car within 21 days of your policy start or renewal date, or
- you provided an invalid odometer reading, such as if the odometer reading of the car at the time of incident is less than the one shown on your most recent Insurance Certificate.
More than one Additional Excess may apply. The total excess you may have to pay is the sum of the Basic Excess and all of the Additional Excesses which apply at the time of the incident.
For more information about excesses that apply to your policy, please refer to our Premium, Excess and Discount Guide which is available on our website or by calling us.
