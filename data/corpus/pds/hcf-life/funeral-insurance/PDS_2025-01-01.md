- your age at the Cover Commencement Date,
- your Insured Benefit amount,
- any applicable discounts,
- our operating costs, specifically our costs of capital, our operating expenses and government charges that apply to this insurance, such as stamp duty.
HOW YOUR PREMIUMS MAY CHANGE Your premium will not change each year as a result of your age.
