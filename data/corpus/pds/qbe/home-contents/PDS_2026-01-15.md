# QBE Home & Contents Insurance (PDS 2026-01-15)

*Jurisdiction: Australia (Cth) | Effective Date: 2026-01-15*

---

### Defined Insured Events, Flood & Theft Conditions
**Clause Classification:** `Coverage`

QBE Home & Contents covers defined events including fire, storm, flood, lightning, theft or burglary, malicious damage, and escape of liquid. Theft from unsecured areas without violent and forcible entry is restricted.

#### CRITICAL POLICY EXCLUSIONS & LIMITATIONS (WHAT IS NOT COVERED):
- 72-hour embargo period applies to bushfire, storm, and flood claims after policy inception.
- Storm surge and action of the sea are excluded unless occurring as part of an insured flood event.

> ⚠️ **Mandatory Embargo Window:** A strict **72-hour embargo** applies post-purchase. No cover for losses occurring within 72 hours of policy inception unless transferring without lapse.
