### Definitions — Action of The Sea
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

**Action of The Sea** means any natural movement or disturbance of sea water, including but not limited to tidal wave, high tide, king tide, Storm Surge, or other abnormal rise or movement of the sea. This definition excludes Tsunami, which is defined separately.

### Definitions — Building(s)
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

**Building(s)** means the Building or Buildings located at the Situation and defined under the applicable Strata Legislation where the property is situated, that you own or are legally responsible for. This includes, but is not limited to:
- Outbuildings, garages, sheds, and other structures;
- Permanent fixtures and structural improvements including external blinds, awnings, gates, fences, retaining walls;
- Fixed or built in plant, equipment and appliances;
- In-ground swimming pools and spas;
- Marinas, wharves, jetties, docks, pontoons, and similar non-commercial facilities (fixed or floating), excluding fuel distribution infrastructure unless otherwise agreed in writing;
- Satellite dishes, antennas, aerials, masts, towers, and associated wiring and footings;
- Underground and overhead services, including pipes, cables, and power poles;
- Tennis courts and recreational surfaces;
- Stratum Lots or Volumetric Lots, where applicable.
Building does not include:
- Illegal installations;
- Mobile or fixed air-conditioning units servicing individual Lots (where excluded by applicable Strata Legislation, such as in Queensland);
- Plants, soil, gravel, mulch, or landscaping materials unless covered under a specific benefit;
- Temporary floor coverings, carpets, curtains, blinds, and window furnishings inside individual Lots;
- Removable fixtures or property for which Tenants or lessees are responsible under a Residential Tenancy Agreement;
- Common Area contents or personal property of Lot owners.

### Definitions — Common Area Contents
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

**Common Area Contents** means unfixed, movable items owned by the Owners Corporation and located within the shared or Common Area of the Situation. This includes, but is not limited to:
- Furniture, furnishings, household goods, light fittings, internal blinds, curtains, and fire extinguishers;
- Freestanding or portable appliances such as dishwashers, washing machines, dryers, and other electrical items;
- Carpets (fixed or unfixed), floor rugs;

### Definitions — Flood
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

**Flood** means the covering of normally dry land by water that has escaped or been released from the normal confines of any of the following:
- a lake (whether or not it has been altered or modified);
- a river (whether or not it has been altered or modified);
- a creek (whether or not it has been altered or modified);
- another natural watercourse (whether or not it has been altered or modified);
- a reservoir;
- a canal; or
- a dam.

#### CRITICAL POLICY EXCLUSIONS & LIMITATIONS (WHAT IS NOT COVERED):
- Actions of the Sea & Storm Surge: Loss or damage caused by high tide, king tides, tidal waves, ocean rise, or actions of the sea is excluded.
- Rainwater Run-off Demarcation: Surface rainwater run-off escaping over normally dry land (rather than from a natural watercourse, lake, canal, reservoir, or dam) is assessed under Insured Event 3 (Storm cover), not Flood.

> ⚠️ **Mandatory Embargo Window:** A strict **72-hour embargo** applies post-purchase. No cover for losses occurring within 72 hours of policy inception unless transferring without lapse.

### Definitions — Storm Surge
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`
