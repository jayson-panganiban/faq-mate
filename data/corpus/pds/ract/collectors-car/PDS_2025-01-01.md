### Collector's Car PDS
**Clause Classification:** `Coverage`

RACT Collectors Car Insurance Collector's Car Insurance - Updated information The section titled 'About No Claim Bonuses' in each Collector's Car Insurance PDS is deleted, as a No Claim Bonus does not apply to Collector's Car Insurance.
You may have noticed that your PDS includes information about No Claim Bonuses which, if applicable, are shown on your current schedule. If you have Collector's Car Insurance, your schedule will not show a No Claims Bonus because it does not apply to this type of insurance. Please contact us if you would like to know more.
This update takes effect from 15 January 2023.
