### Definitions — Excess
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

**Excess** means the amounts(s) You must contribute to the cost of a claim. The amount and types of Excess that apply to Your Policy will be shown on Your Certificate of Insurance.

---

### Definitions — Flood
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

**Flood** means the covering of normally dry land by water that has escaped or been released from the normal confines of any of the following:
- a lake (whether or not it has been altered or modified);
- a river (whether or not it has been altered or modified);
- a creek (whether or not it has been altered or modified);
- another natural watercourse (whether or not it has been altered or modified);
- involves violence against one or more persons;
- involves damage to property;
- endangers life other than that of the person committing the action;
- a reservoir;
- a canal; or
- creates a risk to health or safety of the public or a section of the public; or
- a dam.
- is designed to interfere with or to disrupt an electronic system.

#### CRITICAL POLICY EXCLUSIONS & LIMITATIONS (WHAT IS NOT COVERED):
- Actions of the Sea & Storm Surge: Loss or damage caused by high tide, king tides, tidal waves, ocean rise, or actions of the sea is excluded.
- Rainwater Run-off Demarcation: Surface rainwater run-off escaping over normally dry land (rather than from a natural watercourse, lake, canal, reservoir, or dam) is assessed under Insured Event 3 (Storm cover), not Flood.

> ⚠️ **Mandatory Embargo Window:** A strict **72-hour embargo** applies post-purchase. No cover for losses occurring within 72 hours of policy inception unless transferring without lapse.

### Definitions — Agreed Value
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

**Agreed Value** means the amount We agree to insure Your Vehicle for that is shown on Your Certificate of Insurance. It includes:
- any Modifications, options or Accessories to Your Vehicle;
- GST;
- registration and any compulsory third party insurance; and
- other on-road costs.
It does not include any:
- allowance for dealer profit;
- warranty costs;
- stamp duty; or
- transfer fees.
If You have ‘Third Party Fire and Theft’ cover, this is an amount up to a maximum of $10,000.

### Definitions — Substitute Vehicle
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

**Substitute Vehicle** means a vehicle that You do not own that has been borrowed or hired because Your Vehicle is being repaired, serviced or has broken down but does not include a hire car rented from a commercial rental company.

---

### Definitions — Sum Insured
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

**Sum Insured** means the Agreed Value of Your Vehicle as shown on the Certificate of Insurance (if You are insured for Agreed Value) or the Market Value of Your Vehicle (if You are insured for Market Value). It is the maximum amount We will pay for loss or damage to Your Vehicle under ‘Cover for loss or damage to Your Vehicle’.
If You have ‘Third Party Fire and Theft’ cover, this is an amount up to a maximum of $10,000.

---

### Definitions — Total Loss
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

**Total Loss** means when We decide to pay You the Sum Insured for Your Vehicle. This is usually where We decide that it is uneconomical to repair Your Vehicle or Your Vehicle has been stolen and not recovered.
