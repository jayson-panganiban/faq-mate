## Frequently asked questions about travel insurance

### How do I get a TID travel insurance quote?

All you need to do is click on the green [get a quote](https://allianzassistancetravel.com.au/leisure/tid) button and enter some details such as; trip type, destination(s), trip dates, number of travellers, and age of each traveller. You’ll then go through a quick process where you can select the plan that best suits your travel needs and then add any benefits or add-ons to suit your policy, for example you can add cover for snow sports, adventure activities or cruising, and cancellation limits and base excess.

*Source: <https://www.travelinsurancedirect.com.au/>*

### What should I do if I need to make a claim?

You can [submit your claim online](https://claimmanager.com.au/aga/agreement). Keep all receipts, medical reports, and supporting documents for a smooth claims process.

*Source: <https://www.travelinsurancedirect.com.au/>*

### Can I extend my travel insurance while overseas?

Yes, you may extend your policy while abroad, but terms and conditions apply. It’s best to check with us before your policy expires. A new policy will be issued, certain restrictions may apply. See the [PDS](https://cft-onex-api.agaassistance.com.au/api/document/pds/tid/ "TID Product Disclosure Statement (PDS)") for more information.

*Source: <https://www.travelinsurancedirect.com.au/>*

### Can I ride a motorbike or scooter overseas?

Yes. We will provide cover if the following has been met:

- You’ve purchased an Adventure Pack
- You’re licensed to ride in Australia, or you hold a valid licence in the country you're visiting, if your Australian licence is not recognised
- You always wear a helmet
- You’re the driver of a quad bike and you don’t have any passengers, or you are not a passenger
- You’re not participating in racing, or riding as part of your job or for money.

To learn more about cover for motorbikes, scooters and what is and isn’t covered check out the Adventure Pack section, under Optional Covers in the [PDS](https://cft-onex-api.agaassistance.com.au/api/document/pds/tid/ "TID Product Disclosure Statement (PDS)").

*Source: <https://www.travelinsurancedirect.com.au/>*

### Am I covered for Coronavirus (COVID-19)?

All international plans include cover for overseas emergency assistance and overseas medical and hospital expenses. Depending on your plan, other COVID-related events, such as cancellations and changes to travel arrangements, may also be covered.

*Source: <https://www.travelinsurancedirect.com.au/>*

### What do I do with my original documents when claiming?

When you are completing your [claim online](https://claimmanager.com.au/aga/agreement), we ask you for electronic copies of all documents. However, for all claims, you need to be able to provide us with the originals if we request them.

When booking your travel arrangements, keep your original documents and receipts to make claiming easier if required.

While travelling, keep medical reports or police statements saved or filed safely until after your claim has been finalised. You'll usually need to upload or email clear copies of all documents, and we may request originals later for verification – so don't throw anything away.

If your documents are lost or stolen, provide supporting evidence (e.g., a police report or written confirmation).

Important: Scan or photograph your documents before travelling for easy access if needed overseas.

You can read more about [making a claim](https://claimmanager.com.au/aga/agreement) here.
