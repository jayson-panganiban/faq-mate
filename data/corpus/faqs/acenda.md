## Section: Identity theft

### What is identity theft and how can it affect your life insurance policy?

Identity theft occurs when someone steals your personal information to impersonate you, usually for financial gain. If a person steals your identity, they may be able to gain to unauthorised access to your policy and online portal. This could allow them to make changes and lodge fraudulent claims or payouts.

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.acenda.com.au/support/customer/fraud-and-scams](https://www.acenda.com.au/support/customer/fraud-and-scams)

---

### What can you do if you suspect your identity has been stolen?

If you suspect your identity has been stolen, take these immediate steps:

1. Contact all your financial institutions. They will provide guidance on next steps and may need to block your accounts and cancel your debit or credit cards.
2. Update all passwords for online banking, investments, superannuation, and insurance accounts. If the perpetrator has gained access to your Acenda Life account, call us directly and we will guide you on the next steps.
3. Contact [IDCare](https://www.idcare.org/) (External link). Australia and New Zealand's national identity and cyber support service
4. Report it to the police either in person or [online](https://www.idcare.org/).

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.acenda.com.au/support/customer/fraud-and-scams](https://www.acenda.com.au/support/customer/fraud-and-scams)
