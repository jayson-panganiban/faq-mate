### Definitions — Journey or Trip
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

**Journey or Trip** means your journey from the time when you leave your home to go directly to the place you depart from on your travels and ends when you return to your home on or before the last day of cover shown on your certificate of insurance. Journeys that involve travel solely within Australia will only be covered when your destination is more than 200 kilometres from your home. If less than that, your trip must include at least one night of pre-paid accommodation. There is a specific definition for journey or trip for Overseas Frequent Traveller, Domestic Frequent Traveller, and Already Overseas plans, please refer to the relevant section.
