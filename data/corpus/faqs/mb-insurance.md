# MB INSURANCE Insurance: Official Policy & Claims FAQs

*Statutory Notice: The following FAQs reflect published disclosures by MB INSURANCE. Policy terms are subject to the applicable Product Disclosure Statement (PDS) and Target Market Determination (TMD).*

## Question 1: What is a cover note?
**Insurer:** MB INSURANCE | **ID:** `mb-insurance-001`

### Official Guidance
A cover note is a short-term contract of insurance that provides immediate coverage for your vehicle. It allows you time to submit all required information and payment to obtain your full policy. The duration of the cover note is specified within the document

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.mbinsurance.com.au/faq/?utm](https://www.mbinsurance.com.au/faq/?utm)

---

## Question 2: What if I have not submitted the required additional information and/or made payment by the end of the cover note period?
**Insurer:** MB INSURANCE | **ID:** `mb-insurance-002`

### Official Guidance
It's important to provide all requested information and payment before the cover note expires. Failure to do so will result in the cover note expiring, and coverage will cease. If you anticipate delays, contact MB Insurance in advance to discuss possible solutions.

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.mbinsurance.com.au/faq/?utm](https://www.mbinsurance.com.au/faq/?utm)
