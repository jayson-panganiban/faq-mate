## Question 2: What is 'agreed value' coverage?
**Insurer:** RAYNO INSURANCE | **ID:** `rayno-insurance-002`

### Official Guidance
Agreed value' means that, in the event of a total loss, you will receive a pre-determined amount agreed upon when the policy is taken out, rather than the market value.
