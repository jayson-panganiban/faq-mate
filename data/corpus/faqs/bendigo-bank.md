## Question 6: What if someone other than the nominated driver drives my car?
**Insurer:** BENDIGO BANK | **ID:** `bendigo-bank-006`

### Official Guidance
If they are under 25, the policy won’t cover them unless specified exceptions apply. If over 25, a double excess is applied.
