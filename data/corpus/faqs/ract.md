## Question 2: What is an excess, and when do I have to pay it?
**Insurer:** RACT | **ID:** `ract-002`

### Official Guidance
An excess is the amount you pay when you make a claim. It is payable at the time of the claim.

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.ract.com.au/insurance-and-finance/insurance-claims/insurance-claims-faqs](https://www.ract.com.au/insurance-and-finance/insurance-claims/insurance-claims-faqs)

## Question 6: What happens if my car is a total loss?
**Insurer:** RACT | **ID:** `ract-006`

### Official Guidance

## Question 7: Are new cars replaced if written off?
**Insurer:** RACT | **ID:** `ract-007`

### Official Guidance
