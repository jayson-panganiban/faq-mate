### Definitions — Agreed Value
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

**Agreed Value** means the amount We agree to insure Your Vehicle for that is shown on Your Certificate of Insurance. It includes:
- any Modifications, options or Accessories to Your Vehicle;
- GST;
- registration and any compulsory third party insurance; and
- other on-road costs.
It does not include any:
- allowance for dealer profit;
- warranty costs;
- stamp duty; or
- transfer fees.

**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

**Authorised Rider** means any person listed on the Certificate of Insurance as a rider of Your Vehicle or any person who is riding or in charge of Your Vehicle with Your permission.

### Definitions — Market Value
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

**Market Value** means Our assessment of the value of the vehicle immediately prior to the incident You are claiming for (or at the time of any claim under the ‘Lifetime repair guarantee’ additional benefit) using local market prices and considering the age and condition of the vehicle. It includes:
- any Modifications, options or Accessories to the vehicle;
- GST;
- registration and any compulsory third party insurance; and
- other on-road costs.

### Definitions — Total Loss
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

**Total Loss** means when We decide to pay You the Sum Insured for Your Vehicle. This is usually where We decide that it is uneconomical to repair Your Vehicle or Your Vehicle has been stolen and not recovered.

### Excess
**Clause Classification:** `SubLimit`

As part of the finalisation of Your claim, You must pay the Excess that applies. An Excess is an amount You contribute towards the cost of a claim. Your Certificate of Insurance shows the amount and types of Excess that apply to Your Policy. You may need to pay more than one Excess.
The type of Excess You need to pay depends on what Your claim relates to and who was riding Your Vehicle.
The following Excesses may apply to Your Policy:
Vehicle applies to most claims under the Policy;
Rider applies in addition to the Vehicle Excess when a person specified with a Rider Excess on the Certificate of Insurance is riding Your Vehicle;
