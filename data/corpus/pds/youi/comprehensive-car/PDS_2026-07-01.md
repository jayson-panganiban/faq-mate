# Youi Comprehensive Car Insurance (PDS 2026-07-01)

*Jurisdiction: Australia (Cth) | Effective Date: 2026-07-01*

---

### Comprehensive Cover, Excess Schedule & Driver Exclusions
**Clause Classification:** `SubLimit`

Youi Comprehensive Car Insurance covers accidental loss or damage to your car, third-party property liability up to $20 million, storm, hail, flood, fire, and theft. Multiple excesses may apply concurrently depending on driver age and listing status.

| Excess Type                       | When It Applies                                          |
| :-------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------- |
| Basic Excess                      | Applies to most claims as shown on your Policy Schedule  |
| Age / Inexperienced Driver Excess | Driver under 25 or licensed less than 2 years            |
| Unnamed Driver Excess             | Driver not listed on Policy Schedule at time of incident |
| Unlicensed Driver Exclusion       | No cover if driven by an unlicensed or suspended driver  |

#### CRITICAL POLICY EXCLUSIONS & LIMITATIONS (WHAT IS NOT COVERED):
- No cover when the vehicle is driven by an unlicensed, suspended, or disqualified driver.
- 72-hour embargo applies to bushfire, cyclone, and flood events from policy start.

> ⚠️ **Mandatory Embargo Window:** A strict **72-hour embargo** applies post-purchase. No cover for losses occurring within 72 hours of policy inception unless transferring without lapse.
