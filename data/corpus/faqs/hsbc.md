## Question 1: How do I retrieve my quote?
**Insurer:** HSBC | **ID:** `hsbc-001`

### Official Guidance
You can retrieve your quote by visiting HSBC's insurance website and entering your details. Alternatively, you can contact HSBC customer support for assistance.

## Question 2: Do I need to add a learner driver to my policy?
**Insurer:** HSBC | **ID:** `hsbc-002`

### Official Guidance
If you have a learner driver in your household, it is important to declare this when applying for your insurance. Depending on the circumstances, you may need to add them to your policy.

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.hsbc.com.au/faqs/](https://www.hsbc.com.au/faqs/)

## Question 3: Am I covered if I use my car for ridesharing services like Uber?
**Insurer:** HSBC | **ID:** `hsbc-003`

### Official Guidance
HSBC typically does not provide coverage for vehicles used for ridesharing services like Uber. You should contact them to confirm specific coverage details.

## Question 5: Are there any types of vehicles HSBC does not insure?
**Insurer:** HSBC | **ID:** `hsbc-005`

### Official Guidance
HSBC generally does not insure vehicles that are modified for racing, vehicles used for professional motorsport, or vehicles that are older than a certain age. It's best to check with HSBC for specifics.

## Question 7: What happens if my car is a total loss?
**Insurer:** HSBC | **ID:** `hsbc-007`

### Official Guidance
If your car is declared a total loss, HSBC may compensate you based on the value of the car at the time of the incident, minus any excess charges.

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.hsbc.com.au/faqs/](https://www.hsbc.com.au/faqs/)

## Question 8: Do I get a hire car in the event of a claim?
**Insurer:** HSBC | **ID:** `hsbc-008`

### Official Guidance
HSBC may provide a hire car if your car is involved in an accident or is being repaired after a covered claim, subject to terms and conditions of your policy.

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.hsbc.com.au/faqs/](https://www.hsbc.com.au/faqs/)

---

## Question 9: Can I cancel my policy and get a refund?
**Insurer:** HSBC | **ID:** `hsbc-009`

### Official Guidance
Yes, you can cancel your policy, but whether you receive a refund depends on the timing and the terms of the policy. HSBC may charge a cancellation fee, and a refund is typically only available if you cancel early in the policy period.

*Canonical Source:* [https://www.hsbc.com.au/faqs/](https://www.hsbc.com.au/faqs/)
