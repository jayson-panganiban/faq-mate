### Excesses
**Clause Classification:** `SubLimit`

An excess is the amount you have to pay for each claim.
We treat each incident as a separate claim. There are three types of excess:
yy a standard excess that applies to all claims;
yy an age excess also applies if a driver under 25 years of age was using your car at the time of the incident; and yy an unlisted household member or unlisted regular driver excess also applies if a driver who was a household member or regular driver, and who was not a listed driver on your policy, was using your car at the time of the incident. This includes you.
This excess will not apply to learner drivers as long as another driver listed on the policy is a passenger in your car.
The amounts of the excesses that apply to your policy are shown on your insurance schedule.
When you claim, you’ll need to pay the applicable excess. Payment of the excess doesn’t mean your claim has been accepted.
If we’ve asked you to pay us the excess, but you haven’t paid us, we won’t provide any further benefits under this policy until you do pay. We also may not cover any additional costs that arise because of a delay in you paying us.
Refer to the Third Party Property Damage Insurance Premiums, Excesses & Claims Guide for further information.
If your claim’s not covered (when it’s a no-can-do) We’ll always assess your claim. If you don’t meet your obligations under this policy, or our assessment shows that the incident isn’t covered by this policy, we may decide to refuse your claim - even if we had let you lodge it to begin with. If so, we’ll give you our reasons in writing.
If we refuse your claim and we’ve already paid any monies on it, we may recover those monies from you.

### Definitions — Household member
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

**Household member** means anyone who usually lives with you, or at the address where your car is kept overnight.
This contractual definition applies across all insured events, policy schedules, and general exclusions unless specified otherwise on your Certificate of Insurance.

---

### Definitions — Your insurance schedule
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

**Your insurance schedule** means your latest insurance schedule we have given you. It is an important document that sets out the information you’ve given us, on which we’ve based our decision to insure you, as well as the individual details of your policy. We’ll send you an updated insurance schedule whenever you let us know these details have changed.

### Definitions — Period of cover
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

**Period of cover** means the time from when your policy starts to when it ends (as shown on your insurance schedule).
This contractual definition applies across all insured events, policy schedules, and general exclusions unless specified otherwise on your Certificate of Insurance.

### Definitions — Policy
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

**Policy** means your insurance contract. It is made up of Part 1 and Part 2 of the PDS, any Supplementary PDS, and your latest insurance schedule we have given you.

### Definitions — Regular driver
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

**Regular driver** means anyone who usually drives your car at least once a week, or anyone who is driving your car in connection with ride share services.
Ride share is when your car is used to provide trips or journeys through ride share or ride hail apps.
Examples of ride share or ride hail apps include, but are not limited to, UberX, Lyft and GoCatch.
Cars that have a taxi or hire car licence are not treated as ride share for this policy.
Using your car and use your car and use of your car mean your car being driven, being in the control of, or otherwise being in the care of someone.
