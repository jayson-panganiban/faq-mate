# Domain Glossary: Australian General Insurance FAQ RAG ("FAQ-Mate")

This glossary establishes the canonical, non-negotiable domain language for FAQ-Mate. All specifications, TypeScript interfaces, prompt engineering templates, and evaluation assertions must adhere strictly to these terms.

---

## 1. Statutory Documents & Regulatory Instruments

### Product Disclosure Statement (PDS)
* **Definition:** A legal document required under Part 7.9 of the *Corporations Act 2001 (Cth)* provided by an Australian general insurer to retail clients. It sets out the terms, conditions, standard covers, sub-limits, excess amounts, exclusions, and claims procedures for a specific insurance policy.
* **Invariant:** A PDS is the authoritative contractual baseline. Any coverage assertion generated by the system must be directly grounded in an identified section and page of the active PDS.

### Supplementary Product Disclosure Statement (SPDS)
* **Definition:** A legal amendment issued between major PDS publication cycles that adds, deletes, or alters specific clauses in the primary PDS.
* **Invariant:** An SPDS takes legal precedence over the base PDS for any conflicting clause. Chunks derived from an SPDS must carry an overriding priority metadata tag.

### Target Market Determination (TMD)
* **Definition:** A public document mandated under the *Treasury Laws Amendment (Design and Distribution Obligations and Product Intervention Powers) Act 2019* (DDO regime) describing the class of retail consumers for whom the product was designed, distribution conditions, and review triggers.
* **Invariant:** The system references TMDs exclusively for factual suitability criteria. The system **never** assesses whether a specific individual falls within the target market.

### Financial Services Guide (FSG)
* **Definition:** A document required under Part 7.7 of the *Corporations Act 2001 (Cth)* describing the financial services provided, licensing status, remuneration, and internal/external dispute resolution avenues.

### Curated Insurer Guide (GUIDE)
* **Definition:** An official insurer knowledge base document published online by an issuing underwriter to explain policy concepts, claim steps, peril/event distinctions, and coverage boundaries in consumer-friendly, plain-English terms across Home, Motor, Travel, Life, and Commercial product lines.
* **Role in Tripartite Knowledge Base:** Functions as the conversational explanation layer (`corpus/guides/`), bridging retail consumer queries and legal contractual baseline clauses (`corpus/pds/`).
* **Invariant:** Explanatory guides provide conversational clarity and context, but cannot override binding contractual exclusions or definitions set forth in the statutory PDS/SPDS. Dual-provenance responses synthesize both sources and cite them distinctly with dedicated `GUIDE` and `PDS` provenance badges.

### Issuing Underwriter vs. Distributing Brand (Bancassurance & White-Label)
* **Definition:** In Australian general and life insurance, a retail **Distributing Brand** (such as a bank in a Bancassurance arrangement like CommBank, Westpac, ANZ, or NAB, or a retail challenger like Coles Insurance or Everyday Insurance) markets policies under its own brand name, while a licensed **Issuing Underwriter** (an APRA-authorised insurer holding an Australian Financial Services Licence, such as AAI Limited, Allianz Australia Insurance Limited, Insurance Australia Limited, QBE Insurance (Australia) Limited, or Auto & General Insurance Company Limited) bears the underwriting risk and issues the PDS.
* **Invariant:** Queries referencing a Distributing Brand must resolve to the underlying Issuing Underwriter's operative PDS/SPDS terms while preserving provenance of the underwriter relationship in the document breadcrumb.

## 2. Regulatory Boundaries & Advice Classification

### Factual Information
* **Definition:** Objectively verifiable facts about policy terms, statutory definitions, claims procedures, or product features without value judgment or recommendation.
* **System Policy:** Permitted. This is the sole operating mode of FAQ-Mate.

### General Advice
* **Definition:** Financial product advice that does not take into account the user's personal objectives, financial situation, or needs (*Corporations Act 2001 (Cth)*, Section 766B(4)).
* **System Policy:** Permitted only when accompanied by a persistent **General Advice Warning (GAW)** under Section 949A.

### Personal Advice
* **Definition:** Financial product advice where the provider has considered one or more of the person's objectives, financial situation, or needs, or a reasonable person would consider the provider to have done so (*Corporations Act 2001 (Cth)*, Section 766B(3) and ASIC RG 244).
* **System Policy:** **STRICTLY PROHIBITED.** The system operates without an Australian Financial Services Licence (AFSL). Any user query presenting personal financial framing (e.g., budget, income, personal recommendation requests) must trigger a deterministic refusal and safe-harbor redirection.

### General Advice Warning (GAW)
* **Definition:** The mandatory statutory disclaimer under Section 949A of the *Corporations Act 2001 (Cth)*.
* **Canonical Copy:**
  > *"This information is general product information only and does not take into account your personal objectives, financial situation, or needs. Before making any financial decision, you should read the relevant Product Disclosure Statement (PDS) and Target Market Determination (TMD) and seek independent professional advice."*

## 3. Insurance Coverage Concepts & Prescribed Perils

### Prescribed Flood Definition
* **Definition:** A uniform statutory definition mandated under the *Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth)* and *Insurance Contracts Amendment (Prescribed Flood Definition) Regulations 2012*:
  > *"The covering of normally dry land by water that has escaped or been released from the normal confines of: (a) any lake, or any natural channel, whether or not it has been altered; (b) any reservoir, canal, or dam."*
* **Domain Seam:** Must be distinguished from **Rainwater Run-off** (water pooling on surface during rain) and **Action of the Sea / Storm Surge** (ocean swells/high tides). Policies that cover Storm often exclude Flood unless an explicit Flood cover option is selected.

### Bushfire Embargo Period
* **Definition:** A statutory/contractual exclusion window (typically 48 to 72 hours immediately following policy inception) during which no cover is provided for bushfire, grassfire, or named cyclone damage.
* **Invariant:** Answers concerning newly purchased policies during active bushfire threats must highlight the embargo window and refuse to confirm immediate coverage.

### Sub-Limit
* **Definition:** A specific contractual cap placed on a particular category of item or peril (e.g., jewelry capped at \$2,500 per claim, garden tools capped at \$1,000) that sits below the total Sum Insured.
* **Invariant:** Sub-limits must never be collapsed or omitted when answering queries about high-value item cover.

### Basic Excess
* **Definition:** The initial out-of-pocket amount the policyholder must contribute towards each approved claim before the insurer covers the remainder.

## 4. Evaluation & Compliance Metrics

### AFCA (Australian Financial Complaints Authority)
* **Definition:** The external dispute resolution (EDR) scheme for Australian financial and general insurance disputes.
* **Domain Significance:** Falsely affirming coverage or misquoting an exclusion represents misleading or deceptive conduct under the *ASIC Act 2001* and *Insurance Contracts Act 1984*, creating direct AFCA dispute exposure.

### Groundedness / Faithfulness Score
* **Definition:** The mathematical proportion of factual statements in an LLM completion that are directly verifiable from the retrieved PDS context chunks ($\ge 0.98$ target).
