### Definitions — Building/s
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

**Building/s** means the private domestic residence and other structures at the premises; including permanently attached fixtures, fittings and structural improvements, but excluding earthworks.
Building/s excludes:
- contents items;
- fitted carpets, curtains and other indoor window coverings;
- unsealed or unpaved driveways and paths; for example, dirt or gravel driveways;
- plants, trees and shrubs, garden beds, or horticultural materials;
- grass and lawn;

- any commercial or business premises.

**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

**Flood** means the covering of normally dry land by water that has escaped or been released from the normal confines of any of the following:

- another watercourse (whether or not it has been altered or modified);
- a reservoir;
