### Definitions — Flood
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

Under **Budget Direct Smart Home** (PDS) and the *Insurance Contracts Regulations 2012 (Cth)* Reg 29D standard definition, **Flood** means the covering of normally dry land by water that has escaped or been released from the normal confines of any lake, river, creek, or other natural watercourse (whether or not altered or modified), or any reservoir, canal, or dam.

**Related Water Peril Demarcation:**
- **Storm & Rainwater Run-off:** Surface water or rainwater run-off flowing over normally dry ground during a storm without escaping from a natural watercourse or dam is assessed under **Insured Event 3 (Storm cover)** rather than Flood.
- **Escape of Liquid:** Sudden bursting or leaking from fixed internal pipes, appliances, or hot water systems is assessed under **Insured Event 4 (Escape of Liquid)**.
- **Certificate of Insurance:** Your *Certificate of Insurance* (Policy Schedule) specifies whether Flood cover is included or opted out, along with any applicable Flood or Storm Excess.
- **Official Source URL:** [https://www.autogeneral.com.au/prd/aagwebsite/agca/web/branding/resources/BUDD/legal/home/20160623_SHCPDSB.pdf](https://www.autogeneral.com.au/prd/aagwebsite/agca/web/branding/resources/BUDD/legal/home/20160623_SHCPDSB.pdf)

#### CRITICAL POLICY EXCLUSIONS & LIMITATIONS (WHAT IS NOT COVERED):
- Actions of the Sea & Storm Surge: Loss or damage caused by high tide, king tides, tidal waves, or actions of the sea is excluded.
- 72-Hour Waiting Period (Embargo): No cover applies for flood, storm, bushfire, or cyclone during the first 72 hours of a new policy unless replacing equivalent cover without lapse.
- Retaining walls, sea walls, freestanding outdoor gates/fences, and gradual seepage/hydrostatic pressure are excluded.

> ⚠️ **Mandatory Embargo Window:** A strict **72-hour embargo** applies post-purchase. No cover for losses occurring within 72 hours of policy inception unless transferring without lapse.

### EXCESS ON CLAIMS
**Clause Classification:** `SubLimit`

This is the amount you may have to pay towards each claim.
A basic excess applies to all claims. It will be a standard amount, or you may also be able to alter the excess amount which will alter the premium that you pay.
A fixed excess may be applied to your policy when we decide to accept the risk. This excess cannot be removed.
Additional excesses that may apply:
Earthquake excess will apply to all claims for loss or damage as a result of earthquake Theft/Malicious damage by tenant will apply to all claims for loss or damage as a result of Theft/Malicious damage by your tenants. (Only applies if you purchased the option).
Tenant Default will apply to all claims for loss or damage as a result of absconding tenants. (Only applies if you purchased the option).
If you claim for Home and Contents, you will only need to pay the higher of the basic excesses you selected.
The total excess you have to contribute to a claim is the sum of the basic excess, fixed excess and any additional excesses that may apply. We will advise you of all excess amounts when you apply for the insurance, and the excess amount will be shown on your insurance certificate.
