## About the claims process

### What is a certified copy?

A certified copy is a photocopy of an original document that confirms the original has been sighted and the copy is a true and complete copy of the original. Your documents can be certified by a Justice of the Peace, Commissioner of Affidavits, Notary Public or solicitor, among other officials.

*Source: <https://asteronlife.com.au/claims/claims-faqs>*

### Why do you need my original policy document?

The policy document, including the policy schedule and Memorandum of Transfer, is required as proof that the policy ownership has not been transferred or assigned to another party.

*Source: <https://asteronlife.com.au/claims/claims-faqs>*

### What if I’ve lost my policy document and schedule?

We can still assess your claim, however we’re required under legislation to communicate the loss of the policy and must wait for 10 days before issuing a replacement policy. There may be a policy processing fee payable.

*Source: <https://asteronlife.com.au/claims/claims-faqs>*

### What happens to my policy if the claim is accepted?

Depending on your particular policy, the policy may end once the benefit has been paid. Your claims manager will let you know whether or not that’s the case.

*Source: <https://asteronlife.com.au/claims/claims-faqs>*

### What happens if my claim is declined?

Your claims manager will contact you to explain our decision and reasons.

*Source: <https://asteronlife.com.au/claims/claims-faqs>*

## About your benefits, premiums and tax

### Who will be paid the sum insured?

The benefit will be paid to the policy owner registered on our records.

*Source: <https://asteronlife.com.au/claims/claims-faqs>*

### When will I be paid?

You’ll receive the benefit payment after your claim has been assessed and accepted. Your claims manager will keep you up to date while we’re assessing your claim, so you know how things are going.

*Source: <https://asteronlife.com.au/claims/claims-faqs>*

### How much will I be paid?

If we accept your claim, we’ll pay the sum insured: your claims manager will let you know the amount payable.

*Source: <https://asteronlife.com.au/claims/claims-faqs>*

### How will I be paid?

Benefit payments can be made by cheque or direct credit to your nominated account.

*Source: <https://asteronlife.com.au/claims/claims-faqs>*

### Do I need to pay my policy premiums while my claim is being assessed?

Yes, you need to continue paying premiums while we’re assessing your claim.

*Source: <https://asteronlife.com.au/claims/claims-faqs>*

### Do I need to pay my policy premiums once my claim is accepted and I’ve received my benefit?

Depending on your particular policy, the policy may end once the benefit has been paid. If that’s the case, you don’t need to continue paying premiums and your claims manager will let you know.

*Source: <https://asteronlife.com.au/claims/claims-faqs>*

### Are premiums of my other policies automatically waived?

No, not all policies contain a waiver of premium benefit. Your claims manager can advise you about whether your policy contains a waiver of premium benefit.

*Source: <https://asteronlife.com.au/claims/claims-faqs>*

### Does Asteron Life deduct tax from my benefit payments?

We don’t usually deduct tax, but it’s always a good idea to seek independent tax advice for your personal circumstances.

*Source: <https://asteronlife.com.au/claims/claims-faqs>*

## Claims buy back options

### My policy includes a Buy Back Option. What is Buy Back?

Buy Back lets you repurchase an insurance benefit 12 months after benefits have been paid to you under the policy and, because you’ve previously had cover with us, the Buy Back benefit can be purchased without the need for additional medical information.

The type and amount of cover you can buy back will depend on the specific policy you had previously. Your claims manager will let you know the details of any Buy Back Option you can access.

*Source: <https://asteronlife.com.au/claims/claims-faqs>*

### What is the internal dispute resolution process?

For information about our internal dispute resolution process, please refer to our [Complaints Management Process and Complaints Policy](https://asteronlife.com.au/about-us/complaint-management-process).

*Source: <https://asteronlife.com.au/claims/claims-faqs>*

### What is the external dispute resolution process?

If, after the internal dispute resolution process, you remain dissatisfied with our decision, you can contact the Australian Financial Complaints Authority, who are an independent body that can provide information about the next steps you can take. For more information, please refer to our [Complaints Management Process and Complaints Policy](https://asteronlife.com.au/about-us/complaint-management-process).

*Source: <https://asteronlife.com.au/claims/claims-faqs>*
