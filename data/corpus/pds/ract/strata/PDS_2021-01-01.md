### Definitions — Flood
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

Under **RACT Strata** (PDS) and the *Insurance Contracts Regulations 2012 (Cth)* Reg 29D standard definition, **Flood** means the covering of normally dry land by water that has escaped or been released from the normal confines of any lake, river, creek, or other natural watercourse (whether or not altered or modified), or any reservoir, canal, or dam.

**Related Water Peril Demarcation:**
- **Storm & Rainwater Run-off:** Surface water or rainwater run-off flowing over normally dry ground during a storm without escaping from a natural watercourse or dam is assessed under **Insured Event 3 (Storm cover)** rather than Flood.
- **Escape of Liquid:** Sudden bursting or leaking from fixed internal pipes, appliances, or hot water systems is assessed under **Insured Event 4 (Escape of Liquid)**.
- **Certificate of Insurance:** Your *Certificate of Insurance* (Policy Schedule) specifies whether Flood cover is included or opted out, along with any applicable Flood or Storm Excess.
- **Official Source URL:** [https://www.datocms-assets.com/49357/1626741309-racticdd002-strata-pds-web-september-2021.pdf](https://www.datocms-assets.com/49357/1626741309-racticdd002-strata-pds-web-september-2021.pdf)

#### CRITICAL POLICY EXCLUSIONS & LIMITATIONS (WHAT IS NOT COVERED):
- Actions of the Sea & Storm Surge: Loss or damage caused by high tide, king tides, tidal waves, or actions of the sea is excluded.
- 72-Hour Waiting Period (Embargo): No cover applies for flood, storm, bushfire, or cyclone during the first 72 hours of a new policy unless replacing equivalent cover without lapse.
- Retaining walls, sea walls, freestanding outdoor gates/fences, and gradual seepage/hydrostatic pressure are excluded.

> ⚠️ **Mandatory Embargo Window:** A strict **72-hour embargo** applies post-purchase. No cover for losses occurring within 72 hours of policy inception unless transferring without lapse.

### Cooling-off period
**Clause Classification:** `Coverage`

You have 21 days from the commencement of your policy in which you can cancel it. All you need to do is contact us on 13 27 22 or visit your nearest branch. You do not need to give us a reason. Provided you have not made a claim under the policy, we will cancel it and provide you with a full refund of the premium you have paid. Your sum insured You need to ensure your sum insured is accurate when you insure your strata building and also each time you renew your policy.
To ensure your sum insured is adequate, it is important to review it regularly, being mindful of:
- age, construction, condition, style, layout, features and outbuildings;
- rebuild costs, professional fees and cost of demolition;
- any building work or renovations recently completed at the insured address;
- any items recently purchased.

You can ask us to change the sum insured at any time, for example, following the completion of any strata building renovations. The sum insured for your strata building should not include the value of the land.

Automatic adjustment of your sum insured at renewal When your policy is due for renewal, we may increase your strata building sum insured to allow for any increase in building costs and inflationary trends.
