*Jurisdiction: Australia (Cth) | Effective Date: 2025-05-24*

### Definitions — Flood
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

Under **Suncorp Strata Insurance** (PDS) and the *Insurance Contracts Regulations 2012 (Cth)* Reg 29D standard definition, **Flood** means the covering of normally dry land by water that has escaped or been released from the normal confines of any lake, river, creek, or other natural watercourse (whether or not altered or modified), or any reservoir, canal, or dam.

**Related Water Peril Demarcation:**
- **Storm & Rainwater Run-off:** Surface water or rainwater run-off flowing over normally dry ground during a storm without escaping from a natural watercourse or dam is assessed under **Insured Event 3 (Storm cover)** rather than Flood.
- **Escape of Liquid:** Sudden bursting or leaking from fixed internal pipes, appliances, or hot water systems is assessed under **Insured Event 4 (Escape of Liquid)**.
- **Certificate of Insurance:** Your *Certificate of Insurance* (Policy Schedule) specifies whether Flood cover is included or opted out, along with any applicable Flood or Storm Excess.
- **Official Source URL:** [https://www.suncorp.com.au/content/dam/suncorp/insurance/suncorp-insurance/documents/home-and-contents/strata/suncorp-strata-pds-27877-24-05-25-a.pdf](https://www.suncorp.com.au/content/dam/suncorp/insurance/suncorp-insurance/documents/home-and-contents/strata/suncorp-strata-pds-27877-24-05-25-a.pdf)

#### CRITICAL POLICY EXCLUSIONS & LIMITATIONS (WHAT IS NOT COVERED):
- Actions of the Sea & Storm Surge: Loss or damage caused by high tide, king tides, tidal waves, or actions of the sea is excluded.
- 72-Hour Waiting Period (Embargo): No cover applies for flood, storm, bushfire, or cyclone during the first 72 hours of a new policy unless replacing equivalent cover without lapse.
- Retaining walls, sea walls, freestanding outdoor gates/fences, and gradual seepage/hydrostatic pressure are excluded.

> ⚠️ **Mandatory Embargo Window:** A strict **72-hour embargo** applies post-purchase. No cover for losses occurring within 72 hours of policy inception unless transferring without lapse.

### Definitions — Common contents
**Clause Classification:** `Definition`

**Common contents** means your unfixed property at the insured address that you own or for which you are legally responsible and which is not part of any lot or common property. Common contents may include:
- unfixed wall, floor and ceiling coverings such as floor coverings that were not fitted when the strata building was originally built, whether secured to the floor or not
- internal window coverings
- portable domestic appliances (for example, washing machine and clothes dryer)
- tools and cleaning equipment
- domestic tools and gardening equipment (including ride-on mowers)
- office equipment, electronic equipment, and fixed line telephones you use in your administration office, examples include:
- computers, including laptops, electronic diaries, tablets, printers, and scanners (but not electronic data or files, including software, games, or stored media, unless it meets the condition in the general exclusion ‘Photographs, electronic data, and images’ on page 38)
- filing cabinets
- fax machines and photocopiers
- chairs, tables, desks, and other office furniture
- office stationery.
We don’t cover as your common contents Common contents doesn’t include any of the following:
- property owned by a lot owner or lot occupier
- property for which a lot owner or lot occupier is legally responsible
- any fixture or item owned by a lot owner or lot occupier which they could legally remove if they sold or vacated their unit or lot
- personal effects and valuables designed to be worn or carried by a person such as clothing, footwear, baggage, handbags, wallet, jewellery and watches
- valuable items such as:
- cash, smart cards, phone cards, documents able to be cashed or traded, vouchers, money orders, or stamps
- medals, collections, or memorabilia
- precious stones
